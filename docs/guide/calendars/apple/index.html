Connect Apple Calendar

Apple Calendar is accessed through CalDAV, which requires an app-specific password for security. This lets BusyTime connect directly to your iCloud calendar.

Create an app-specific password

  1. Open Apple Account settings and sign in with your Apple ID.
  2. Go to the Sign-In and Security (or Security) section.
  3. Under App-Specific Passwords, click Generate Password (details).
  4. Enter a label (for example, “BusyTime”) and click Create.
  5. Copy the generated password.

Add it in Configure

  1. Open the Configure tool and go to Calendar Providers.
  2. Choose Apple iCloud and click Add Provider.
  3. Enter your iCloud email address as the username and the app-specific password as the password.
  4. Enter the names of the calendars you want to track.
  5. Continue to Device setup to save the settings.
