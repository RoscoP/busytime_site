How to connect BusyTime to a work calendar

Work calendars will often be limited on what programs can access them. This can usually be worked around by sharing a limited view of the calendar with your personal account.

Google

  1. Open Google Calendar.

  2. Click Settings Gear -> Settings.

  3. Click Calendar Name (BusyTime in this example).

  4. Click Shared with -> Add people and groups.

  5. Enter your personal Gmail address.

  6. Check your email and accept the calendar.

  7. The calendar is now selectable when configuring BusyTime.

  8. See Google’s Share your calendar with someone.

How to connect BusyTime to Apple Calendar

Apple Calendar can be accessed through CalDAV authentication, which requires an app-specific password for security. This allows BusyTime to connect directly to your iCloud calendar.

Apple Calendar

  1. Open iCloud Account Settings.
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Navigate to the Security section.
  4. Under App-Specific Passwords, click Generate Password (details).
  5. Enter a label (for example, “BusyTime”) and click Create.
  6. Copy the generated app-specific password.
  7. On the Configure page, select Configure Apple Calendar.
  8. Enter your iCloud email address as the username.
  9. Use the app-specific password you just generated.
  10. Enter the names of the calendars you want to track.
