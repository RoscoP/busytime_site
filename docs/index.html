BusyTime

Lights for Busy Times

BusyTime is a light that displays your busy status according to your selected Google, Outlook, Apple, Yahoo, CalDAV or ICS calendars. It is powered over USB-C and shows a color indicating whether you are currently busy.

Setup requires a PC or Mac with a USB connection to the USB-C port on the device. After setup, the device requires only USB-C power and can be placed anywhere with WiFi access.

Configure BusyTime Setup guide

Status colors

ColorStateMeaning
Free No busy events.
Free, scheduled Scheduled event that is marked free (for example, all-day events).
Upcoming A busy time is coming up.
Busy, more to follow Currently busy, and another busy time follows.
Busy Currently busy.
Error The device cannot read the calendar. The light pulses between blue and red.
Off No upcoming busy times, or outside the checking hours.

Setup

  1. Connect BusyTime to a PC or Mac with a USB-C cable. It appears as a removable drive.
  2. Go to Configure, and select your calendars and WiFi network.
  3. Save and download your `settings.json` file to the BusyTime device drive
  4. Place the device anywhere with WiFi access. It requires only USB-C power.
