---
title: Calendar Setup
description: Prepare Google, work or Apple calendars so BusyTime can track them.
---

BusyTime tracks the calendars you add in the **Calendar Providers** section of the [Configure tool](/configure). Some calendars need a one-time step first.

| Your calendar | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Personal Google Calendar | [Connect Google Calendar](/guide/calendars/google) |
| Work Google Calendar that blocks other apps | [Share it with a personal account](/guide/calendars/google-work-share) |
| Apple (iCloud) Calendar | [Create an app-specific password](/guide/calendars/apple) |

When you are done, continue with [Device setup](/guide/device).
