---
title: Connect Google Calendar
description: Connect a Google Calendar to BusyTime with Google sign-in.
---

Google Calendar uses Google sign-in, so no password is stored in your settings.

1. Open the [Configure tool](/configure) and go to **Calendar Providers**.
1. Choose **Google Calendar** and click **Add Provider**.
1. Sign in with your Google account and allow BusyTime to read your calendar.
1. Enter the names of the calendars you want to track.
1. Continue to [Device setup](/guide/device) to save the settings.

If the calendar belongs to a work account that does not allow other apps, [share it with a personal account](/guide/calendars/google-work-share) first and connect with that account.
