---
title: Track a Work Google Calendar
description: Share a work Google Calendar with your personal account so BusyTime can track it.
---

Work calendars are often limited in which programs can access them. This can usually be worked around by sharing a limited view of the calendar with your personal account, then [connecting that account to BusyTime](/guide/calendars/google).

1. Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) with your work account.
1. Click **Settings Gear** -> **Settings**.

   ![Google Calendar settings menu](/img/CalendarSettings.png)

1. Click **Calendar Name** (BusyTime in this example).

   ![Selecting the calendar in settings](/img/CalendarSelect.png)

1. Click **Shared with** -> **Add people and groups**.

   ![Share with specific people section](/img/CalendarShare.png)

1. Enter your *personal* Gmail address.

   ![Entering an email address in the share dialog](/img/CalendarShareWithEmail.png)

1. Check your personal email and accept the calendar.

   ![Email invitation to accept the shared calendar](/img/EmailAcceptShare.png)

1. The calendar is now selectable when you [connect Google Calendar](/guide/calendars/google) with your personal account.

See Google's [Share your calendar with someone](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37082).
