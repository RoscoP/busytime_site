---
title: BusyTime
description: BusyTime is a light that displays your busy status according to your calendar.
---

BusyTime is a light that displays your busy status according to your selected Google, Outlook, Apple, Yahoo, CalDAV or ICS calendars. It is powered over USB-C and shows a [color](/guide/status-colors) indicating whether you are currently busy.

Setup requires a PC or Mac with a USB connection to the USB-C port on the device. After setup, the device requires only USB-C power and can be placed anywhere with WiFi access.
